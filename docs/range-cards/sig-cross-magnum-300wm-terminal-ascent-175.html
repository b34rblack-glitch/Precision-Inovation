Before you trust it

  • Chronograph the load. 3,030 fps is Federal's 24 in test-barrel number. Your CROSS also has a 24 in barrel, but individual rifles commonly run 20 to 60 fps off. Type the real average into Muzzle velocity.
  • Measure sight height. 2.1 in is 1.25 in (ALPHA6 extra-high, rail top to optic centerline, per SIG) plus about 0.85 in from bore center to the top of the CROSS's integral rail, which is an estimate. Measure bore center to scope-tube center and enter it. Each 0.1 in only moves the 1,000 yd hold about 0.03 MIL, so this is a small term.
  • True it at distance. Confirm the 100 yd zero in the conditions you zeroed, then shoot 500 to 600 yd on a known target. If impacts are high or low, adjust Muzzle velocity until the predicted hold matches, then re-check 800 or beyond.
  • Temperature. The condition presets change air density only. They do not change powder speed, because Federal does not publish the load's temperature sensitivity. If your summer and winter chrono numbers differ, enter each.

Model

  • Point-mass RK4 solver with the McCoy G7 reference drag curve, ICAO atmosphere with humidity, the same engine as the Precision Innovation app. Zero is solved on flat ground; incline rotates gravity for the shot only.
  • Spin drift uses the Miller stability factor and Litz's approximation for a 1.466 in, 175 gr bullet in a 1:9 right-hand twist. Coriolis and aerodynamic jump are not included; both are under 0.1 MIL inside 800 yd for this load.
  • The hold tiles and the table round to 0.1 MIL, the Mark 4HD's click value. The chart and tooltip use unrounded values.
  • Published figures: Federal lists P300WTA2 at 3,030 fps with a G7 BC of .258 and a 175 gr Terminal Ascent bullet 1.466 in long. SIG lists the CROSS Magnum with a 24 in 1:9 barrel and the ALPHA6 extra-high rings at 1.25 in.

This is a prediction, not a confirmation. Mark confirmed holds on the printed card and let them override the table.
