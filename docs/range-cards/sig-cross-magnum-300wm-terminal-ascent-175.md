## Before trusting it

- Chronograph the load and replace the published 3,030 fps; rifles commonly run 20 to 60 fps off the test barrel.
- Measure sight height (bore center to scope-tube center). 2.1" is 1.25" of ring plus an estimated 0.85" from bore to the top of the CROSS rail.
- Confirm the 100 yd zero, then true muzzle velocity against impacts at 500 to 600 yd before relying on holds past that.
- Condition sets change air density only; the load's powder temperature sensitivity is not published and is not modeled.

Sources: Federal P300WTA2 product listing (3,030 fps, G7 .258, G1 .520, 1.466" bullet); SIG SAUER CROSS Magnum specs (24" 1:9 barrel); SIG ALPHA6 34 mm rings (extra high 1.25").
